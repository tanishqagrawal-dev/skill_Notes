Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SKiL MATRiX and our intelligent tools.

What is SKiL MATRiX?

+

SKiL MATRiX is an advanced, all-in-one academic and coding platform designed for engineering students. We provide curated notes, competitive coding environments, AI-assisted learning tools, and comprehensive performance tracking to help you excel in your career.

Is the platform free to use?

+

Yes! Our "Free Tier" plan is completely free and gives you access to unlimited note viewing, basic dashboard features, the free Coding Arena, and limited AI queries. For advanced features, we offer the "CodeTantra Hub" and "Premium Scholar" plans.

What is the Coding Arena?

+

The Coding Arena is a competitive programming environment integrated directly into our platform. You can solve curated problems, compile and run code in multiple languages right in your browser, and climb the Global Leaderboard!

How does the AI Coach work?

+

Our AI Coach is a specialized language model trained on engineering curricula. It provides instant, step-by-step explanations for complex doubts, making it feel like you have a personal tutor available 24/7.

What is the CodeTantra Hub?

+

CodeTantra Hub is a premium subscription tier that unlocks exclusive, detailed lab solutions and study guides. It's tailored specifically to help you ace your CodeTantra assignments with ease.

Can the CGPA Analyzer predict future grades?

+

Yes! The CGPA Analyzer allows you to input your current results and set targets. It then calculates exactly what SGPA you need in upcoming semesters to reach your goal, helping you plan your study efforts effectively.

What are NeuroSprint Pro and Attendance Pro?

+

NeuroSprint Pro is an advanced productivity timer that uses a customized Pomodoro technique to help you maintain deep focus. Attendance Pro tracks your lecture history and tells you exactly how many classes you can afford to miss while staying above 75%.

How do Referral Codes work?

+

When you create an account, you get a unique referral code. By sharing it with your friends, you can both earn exclusive rewards and even unlock premium subscription features for free!

What is included in the Premium Scholar plan?

+

Premium Scholar includes unlimited AI Model Paper generation, unlimited AI Coach access, deep performance analytics, a verified "Scholar" badge, priority support, and a completely ad-free experience across the platform.

Can I generate my own Model Papers?

+

Yes! Our AI-powered Model Paper Generator creates custom practice exams based on your syllabus. It analyzes previous year patterns and focuses on high-weightage topics to maximize your exam readiness.

How does the Global Leaderboard work?

+

You earn XP by solving problems in the Coding Arena and actively participating on the platform. The top students are featured on the global leaderboard, giving you ultimate bragging rights and a competitive edge!

Is my academic data secure?

+

Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your personal information. Your learning data is strictly confidential and used solely to personalize your experience and improve our recommendations.
