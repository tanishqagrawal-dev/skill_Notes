Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SKiL MATRiX and our intelligent tools.

What is SKiL MATRiX Notes?

+

SKiL MATRiX Notes is an advanced academic platform designed for students. We provide curated notes, AI-assisted learning tools, and comprehensive performance tracking to help you excel in your exams.

Is the platform free to use?

+

Yes! Our "Free Tier" plan is completely free and gives you access to unlimited note viewing, basic dashboard features, and limited AI queries. We also offer a "Scholar PRO" plan for advanced features.

What tools are available in the Notes Hub?

+

The Notes Hub provides curated engineering notes, previous year papers, and a model paper generator. It also includes a personal bookmarking system to keep your most important resources organized in one place.

How does the AI Coach work?

+

Our AI Coach is a specialized language model trained specifically on engineering curricula. It provides instant, step-by-step explanations for complex doubts, making it feel like you have a personal tutor available 24/7.

Can I upload my own notes?

+

Currently, note uploads are by invitation or selection only to ensure quality. However, we are launching a "Contributor" program soon where top students can share their notes and earn rewards.

Can the CGPA Analyzer predict future grades?

+

Yes! The CGPA Analyzer allows you to input your current results and set targets. It then calculates exactly what SGPA you need in upcoming semesters to reach your goal, helping you plan your study efforts effectively.

What is NeuroSprint Pro?

+

NeuroSprint Pro is an advanced productivity timer integrated directly into your dashboard. It uses a customized Pomodoro technique to help you maintain deep focus, complete with session tracking and break reminders.

How does Attendance Pro keep me safe?

+

Attendance Pro tracks your lecture history and calculates your current percentage. It tells you exactly how many more classes you can skip while staying above 75%, or how many you must attend to recover your attendance.

Are AI Model Papers unique for every subject?

+

Absolutely. Our generator analyzes your specific branch and semester syllabus to create custom practice exams. These papers focus on high-weightage topics and frequently asked patterns to maximize your exam readiness.

What is included in the Scholar PRO plan?

+

Scholar PRO includes unlimited AI Model Paper generation, deep performance analytics, a verified badge, priority support, and a completely ad-free experience for just ₹99/month.

Is my data secure?

+

Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your personal information. Your learning data is used solely to personalize your experience and improve our recommendations.
